- ❌ **Script Does Not Load After Injection**
  The executor may not be fully attached to the Roblox process. Close Roblox completely, relaunch it, rejoin a **Royale High** server, and attempt injection again with a fresh session.
  **Solution:** Restart both the executor and Roblox, then re-paste the **Royale High Script Pastebin** code and execute.

- ❌ **Diamond Counter Stuck at Zero**
  This usually means the **Royale High Script Diamonds** module could not detect the in-game UI elements after a Roblox update.
  **Solution:** Update to the latest version of the **Royale High Script** — the diamond detection hooks are patched within hours of any Roblox UI change.

- ❌ **Auto Farm Stops After a Few Minutes**
  The **Royale High Script Auto Farm** may be hitting an unhandled teleport cooldown or a server-side rate limit.
  **Solution:** Increase `LoopDelay` in the config to `1.2` or higher, and make sure `AntiDetection` is enabled.

- ❌ **Overlay Not Visible on Mobile**
  The stats dashboard requires overlay permission on Android and a properly signed profile on iOS.
  **Solution:** On Android, enable `Display Over Other Apps` in system settings. On iOS, re-sign the executor app via `AltStore` or `Sideloadly`.

- ❌ **Candy Farm Not Collecting Items**
  The **Royale High Candy Farm Script** only activates during live candy events — it will idle if no event is detected.
  **Solution:** Confirm the candy event is currently active in-game, then toggle `CandyFarmLoop` to `true` in the config table.
